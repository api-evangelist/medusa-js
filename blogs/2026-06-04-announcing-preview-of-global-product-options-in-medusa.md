Medusa has introduced global product options as a preview feature, allowing administrators to create reusable options like Size or Color that apply across multiple products rather than being scoped to individual products. The feature is being released as a preview to gather user feedback before it becomes a stable offering. This enhancement streamlines catalog management for merchants with large product catalogs where shared attributes need consistent configuration across many items.
